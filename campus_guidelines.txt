Sri Vasavi Engineering College - Academic Guidelines

Examination Rules:
Students must carry their hall ticket to every examination. Hall tickets are issued one week before the examination begins. A student who misses more than 25% of classes in a subject is not eligible to write the end semester exam for that subject. Revaluation requests must be submitted within 7 days of result announcement, along with the prescribed fee.

Attendance Policy:
A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory to be eligible for semester examinations. Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may be granted condonation on medical grounds with proper documentation submitted to the Head of Department.

Library Rules:
The library is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on all working days. Students can borrow up to 3 books at a time for a period of 14 days. A fine of Rs. 2 per day is charged for overdue books. Lost books must be replaced or paid for at twice the book's cost.

Hostel Rules:
Hostel curfew time is 9:30 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on weekends. Students must sign the register at the security desk when leaving or entering after 8:00 PM. Mess fees are collected at the start of each semester and are non-refundable after the first two weeks of the semester.

Fee Payment:
Semester fees must be paid within the first 15 days of semester commencement. A late fee of Rs. 500 per week applies after the deadline. Students can pay fees online through the college portal or in person at the accounts office between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM.
